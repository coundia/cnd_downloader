# User Guide

Welcome to CND IPTV Downloader! This guide will help you get started with watching your favorite content.

## Getting Started

### 1. Adding a Playlist
To start watching, you need to add an IPTV source (M3U playlist or Xtream Codes).

1.  Open the **Settings** tab.
2.  Click on **Add Source**.
3.  Choose your method:
    -   **M3U URL**: Enter a name and the link to your M3U file.
    -   **Xtream Codes**: Enter your Server URL, Username, and Password.
4.  Click **Save**. The app will download and process your playlist in the background.

### 2. watching Content
Use the bottom navigation bar to switch between categories:

-   **TV**: Live television channels.
-   **Movies**: On-demand movies.
-   **Series**: TV shows and series.

### 3. Features

#### Search & Filter
-   Use the **Search Bar** at the top to find specific channels or movies.
-   Use the **Filter Chips** (below search) to filter by **Category** (Group) or **Server** (Host).
-   Sort content by Name, Rating, or Date using the sort button.

#### Favorites
-   Click the **Star icon** on any item to add it to your Favorites.
-   Your favorites will appear at the top of the list for quick access.

#### Viewing Modes
-   Toggle between **List View** and **Grid View** using the icons in the top right corner of Movies/Series pages.

#### Playback & Actions
Clicking on an item gives you several options:
-   **Play**: Watch directly in the built-in player.
-   **Details**: View more information (IMDb rating, etc.).
-   **External**: Open the stream in your default media player (e.g., VLC).
-   **Download**: Download the content to your device (if supported).

## Troubleshooting

-   **Stream not loading?** Check your internet connection or try opening it with an External Player.
-   **App slow?** Large playlists are processed in the background. Give it a moment after adding a new source.
